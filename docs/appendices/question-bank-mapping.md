## Domain Score Calculation

### Borrowing & Credit Domain Score

```
Score = (Correct in Q1-Q10) / 10 × 100%
```

### Risk Management Domain Score

```
Score = (Correct in Q11-Q14) / 4 × 100%
```

### Investment & Risk Domain Score

```
Score = (Correct in Q29-Q40) / 12 × 100%
```

### Overall Knowledge Score

```
Score = (Correct in all 26 scored items) / 26 × 100%
```

---

## SDM-10 Eligibility

Only the 26 scored knowledge items (Q1-Q14, Q29-Q40) are eligible for SDM-10 follow-up selection. The 14 preference items (Q15-Q28) do not trigger adaptive follow-up because they assess attitudes rather than factual knowledge.
