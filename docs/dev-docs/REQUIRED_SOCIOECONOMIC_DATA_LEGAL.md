# Required Socioeconomic Data: Legal Considerations

## Is It Legally OK to Make Socioeconomic Data Required?

**Short Answer**: **Yes, it is legally acceptable** to make socioeconomic data required, **especially if you include "Prefer not to say" options** for sensitive fields.

---

## Legal Framework

### 1. **Research Justification** ✅

Your research study explicitly requires socioeconomic data:
- **RQ2 (Heterogeneity)**: "Which baseline behavioral and contextual variables predict heterogeneity in learning gains across students, and do these predictors differ by domain?"
- The independent study document states: "Baseline measures collected alongside the second-week administration capture student characteristics and circumstances plausibly associated with learning trajectories, including demographic and socioeconomic background..."

**Conclusion**: Socioeconomic data is **necessary for your research objectives** (heterogeneity analysis), which justifies requiring it.

### 2. **FERPA Compliance** ✅

Socioeconomic data collection is generally permitted under FERPA when:
- ✅ Data is used for legitimate educational/research purposes
- ✅ Data is protected and access is restricted
- ✅ Students are informed about data usage
- ✅ Data is anonymized for research analysis

**Your implementation**: ✅ All of the above are met.

### 3. **IRB Requirements** ⚠️

For required data collection, IRB protocols typically require:
- **Justification**: Why the data is necessary (you have this - heterogeneity analysis)
- **Minimization**: Only collect necessary data (your fields are relevant)
- **Sensitivity considerations**: Provide alternatives for sensitive information (you have "prefer not to say" options)
- **Transparency**: Inform students about why data is required

**Action**: Ensure your IRB protocol explicitly states that socioeconomic data is required for heterogeneity analysis.

### 4. **Required Course Assignment** ✅

Since this is a **required course assignment**:
- Requiring completion of all sections is generally permissible
- Students are participating as part of coursework
- Research data collection is integrated into the assignment
- Alternatives are available through instructor contact (as stated in your acknowledgment)

## Best Practice: "Required but with 'Prefer Not to Say' Options"

### Recommended Approach

Make the section **required** (students must respond), but include **"Prefer not to say"** as a valid option for sensitive fields:

- ✅ **Required**: Students must answer each question
- ✅ **Respectful**: "Prefer not to say" is available for sensitive information
- ✅ **Completes dataset**: Even "prefer not to say" responses provide data (non-response patterns)
- ✅ **Legally sound**: Respects student autonomy while requiring participation

**Recommended language for IRB proposal**:

All socioeconomic fields are required for completion of the assessment, but students may select 'Prefer not to say' for any sensitive fields. This approach ensures complete datasets for heterogeneity analysis while respecting student autonomy regarding disclosure of sensitive personal information. Non-response patterns (students selecting 'Prefer not to say') are themselves informative for understanding data completeness and potential biases in socioeconomic data collection."
