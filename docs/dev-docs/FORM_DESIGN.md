# Form Design

## Overview

The Financial Literacy Assessment consists of a fixed 40-item anchor assessment administered identically at pre-course and post-course, plus a 10-item Supplemental Diagnostic Module (SDM-10).

## Assessment Structure

| Component | Items | Scored |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Baseline Covariates (Onboarding) | 13 (B1-B13) | No |
| Anchor Assessment | 40 (Q1-Q40) | 26 knowledge items scored |
| SDM-10 (Adaptive) | 10 | Secondary diagnostic |
| **Total** | **63** | **26 primary scored items** |

## Pre/Post Design

- **Pre-course**: Administered in second week of class
- **Post-course**: Administered on last day of class
- **Identical anchors**: All 40 anchor items appear in both administrations
- **SDM-10**: Selected based on individual student's correctness + confidence

## Item Types

### Multiple Choice (MCQ)
- 3-5 answer options
- Single correct answer (for knowledge items)
- Auto-scored against key

### True/False
- Binary response
- Used for foundational concepts
- Auto-scored

### Open-ended (Short Text)
- 1-2 sentence responses
- Used in SDM-10 for diagnostic purposes
- Rubric-based scoring (NLP-assisted)

### Confidence Rating
- **1-3 scale**: Low, Mid, High
- Paired with every anchor item
- Used for calibration analysis and SDM selection

## Question Categories

### Baseline Covariates (B1-B13) - Not Scored
- **B1-B5**: Demographic Characteristics (5 items)
- **B6-B10**: Financial Background & Context (5 items)
- **B11-B13**: Student Loan Debt Status (3 items)
  - B11: Do you have student loan debt?
  - B12: Interest rate (if yes)
  - B13: Maturity/time until repaid (if yes)

### Borrowing, Interest Rates, and Financial Numeracy (Q1-Q10) - 10 items, All Scored
- Compound Interest (Q1)
- Borrowing/Mortgages (Q2)
- Inflation (Q3, Q6, Q7)
- Borrowing/Interest (Q4)
- Saving (Q5)
- Borrowing (Q8)
- Earning (Q9)
- Borrowing/Credit (Q10)

### Behavioral and Risk Management (Q11-Q28) - 18 items
**Knowledge items (Q11-Q14) - Scored:**
- Risk Diversification (Q11, Q14)
- Insurance (Q12, Q13)

**Preference items (Q15-Q28) - NOT Scored:**
- Allocation Preference (Q15)
- Loss Aversion (Q16)
- Risk Perception (Q17)
- Social Influence/Herding (Q18)
- Retirement Risk Planning (Q19)
- Emotional Response to Loss (Q20)
- Decision Process (Q21)
- Risk Confidence (Q22)
- Risk Attitude (Q23)
- Reaction to Underperformance (Q24)
- Definition of Success (Q25)
- Downside Awareness (Q26)
- Risk Preference in Income (Q27)
- Scam Skepticism (Q28)

### Risk and Return Knowledge (Q29-Q40) - 12 items, All Scored
- Investing (Q29, Q30, Q31, Q32)
- Basic Probability (Q33)
- Diversification Effect (Q34)
- Risk-Return Relationship (Q35)
- Diversification Principle (Q36)
- Insurance (Q37)
- Inflation Risk (Q38)
- Asset Class Risk (Q39)
- Crisis/Systemic Risk (Q40)

## Time Limits

- **Recommended completion**: ~20 minutes for anchor assessment
- **No strict time limit**: Students may take as needed
- **Time tracking**: Platform records duration for analysis

## Randomization

- Items presented in consistent sequence (not randomized)
- Ensures domain-based administration
- Supports pre/post comparison

## Accessibility

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance target
- Mobile responsive design
- Clear typography and contrast
- Keyboard navigation support
